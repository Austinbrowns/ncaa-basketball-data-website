March Madness factors

Measure what travels in March.

The four primary March factors are effective FG%, turnover rate, offensive-rebound rate, and free-throw rate. ORB rate uses ORB ÷ (ORB + opponent defensive rebounds); free-throw rate uses FTA ÷ FGA. Context measures remain available for scouting detail.
