Why the data matters

A final score tells us who won. A panel of box scores helps explain how the game keeps being won.

This report treats a team’s box score in a particular game as the observation. Across five seasons, that creates enough repeated evidence to separate a one-night story from a pattern: which advantages travel, which styles produce efficiency, and which small margins decide thousands of games.

The formula

logit(p) = intercept + weighted factor z-scores

Each z-score asks how far a team’s regular-season profile sits above or below the typical postseason team-season. Positive coefficients raise the estimated chance; negative coefficients lower it. Pace, raw points, steals, blocks, and three-point volume remain useful style measures but are not treated as standalone model factors here. The coefficients are generated by scripts/fit-march-model.py from the published CSV.
